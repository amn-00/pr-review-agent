## Your lane: correctness

Find bugs and performance problems in the changed lines.

- category "bug": wrong results, crashes, unhandled errors or edge cases (empty input, None/null, zero, off-by-one), wrong conditions, resource leaks, race conditions.
- category "performance": needless work that matters, such as queries or I/O inside loops, or quadratic algorithms on input that grows.

Don't report style, naming or security problems. Other reviewers cover those.
